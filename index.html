TextCatch

From spoken word to written text — powered by Whisper AI.

New

Your Speech. Your Text. Your Mac.

TextCatch leverages the power of OpenAI's Whisper model to transcribe video, audio, and live recordings into accurate text — all running locally on your Mac. No internet required, no data uploaded, no subscription needed.

Whisper AI 100% Offline macOS Native SRT Export
🤖

Whisper AI Engine

Powered by OpenAI Whisper — the industry-leading open-source speech recognition model. Runs entirely on your Mac for unmatched accuracy.

🎬

Wide Format Support

Import video (MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, M4V, WMV…) or audio files (MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, M4A, OGG…) and start transcribing in seconds.

🎙️

Built-in Recording

Record audio directly inside the app and transcribe it on the spot — perfect for meetings, lectures, or voice memos.

⚙️

Flexible Model Selection

Choose from Whisper's full model range — tiny, base, small, medium, and large — to balance transcription speed against accuracy for your task.

⚡

Fast Local Processing

Everything runs on-device. No cloud uploads, no waiting for a server. Your files stay private and transcription finishes fast.

🖥️

Video Preview

Preview your video directly inside TextCatch. Play back, seek, and review content before or after transcription without leaving the app.

📄

Export SRT & Plain Text

Save transcripts as standard SRT subtitle files ready for video editors, or export as clean plain text for documents, notes, and more.

🌍

Multi-language Support

Transcribe content in dozens of languages. Whisper's multilingual models handle a wide variety of languages, accents, and dialects with ease.

🔒

Privacy First

Your audio and video never leave your machine. TextCatch processes everything locally — no accounts, no data collection, no compromise.
