About Debug Studio

Building tools that make everyday Mac tasks effortless.

Who We Are

Debug Studio is a passionate development studio focused on creating elegant, high-quality software for macOS. Our mission is to solve real-world problems with tools that feel natural and delightful to use.

We believe in clean design, intuitive interfaces, and powerful functionality delivered without complexity. Every product we ship reflects a commitment to craft — minimal UI, fast performance, and genuine respect for the user's time.

As we grow, we continue to expand our lineup of applications, listening to our users and refining our work. Your feedback helps us push the boundaries of what everyday Mac software can be.

Our Apps

TextCatch — Transcribe video, audio, and live recordings into text using OpenAI's Whisper AI model. Runs entirely offline on your Mac. Export as SRT subtitles or plain text.

AudioCatch — Extract audio from any video file with full control over format, bitrate, channels, and sample rate. Available now on the Mac App Store.
