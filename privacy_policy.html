Privacy Policy

Debug Studio — AudioCatch & TextCatch  ·  Effective Date: April 2025

Overview

At Debug Studio, your privacy is a core principle — not an afterthought. This policy applies to all apps published by Debug Studio, including AudioCatch and TextCatch.

The short version: we collect nothing. All processing happens on your device. Your files, recordings, and transcripts never leave your Mac.

Data We Do Not Collect

None of our apps collect, transmit, store, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, account details)
  • Usage data, analytics, or crash reports sent to external servers
  • Audio, video, or media files of any kind
  • Transcription results or generated text
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Any data through third-party advertising or tracking SDKs

We have no backend servers that receive data from our apps. There is no user account system, no login, and no cloud sync — by design.

AudioCatch

AudioCatch converts video files to audio entirely on your Mac. The app reads the video file you select and writes the output audio file to a location you choose. No file content, metadata, or conversion history is transmitted anywhere.

AudioCatch does not:

  • Upload your video or audio files to any server.
  • Store a history of files you have converted.
  • Access files outside of what you explicitly open in the app.
  • Require an internet connection to function.

TextCatch

TextCatch transcribes video, audio, and microphone recordings into text using OpenAI's Whisper model, which runs entirely on your Mac. No audio or transcript data is sent to OpenAI or any other service.

TextCatch does not:

  • Send audio, video, or recording data to any external server or API.
  • Use a cloud-based speech recognition service.
  • Store transcription results outside of what you explicitly export or save.
  • Access your microphone except when you actively start a recording session within the app.
  • Retain recordings after you close or clear them.

All Whisper models used by TextCatch are downloaded and stored locally on your device. Model downloads occur only once, directly from the model source, and contain no user data.

Permissions

Our apps request only the system permissions they need to function:

  • File access — to read the media files you open and write output to the location you choose.
  • Microphone access (TextCatch only) — to capture audio when you start a recording session. This permission is requested at first use and can be revoked at any time in System Settings.

No other permissions are requested or required.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. There are no embedded SDKs from third parties that could collect data on our behalf.

The only external interaction is the one-time download of Whisper model weights in TextCatch. This request contains no user data — it is a standard file download, equivalent to downloading any file in a web browser.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because we collect no data whatsoever, there is no special handling required for younger users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our apps after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
