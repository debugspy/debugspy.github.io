Frequently Asked Questions

Does TextCatch require an internet connection?
No. All transcription is handled entirely on your Mac using local Whisper models. Your files never leave your device.

Which Whisper model should I use in TextCatch?
The small or medium model offers a good balance of speed and accuracy for most use cases. Use large for the highest accuracy, or tiny / base for the fastest results.

What video formats does AudioCatch support?
AudioCatch supports MP4, MKV, MPEG, AVI, WMV, ASF, FLV, F4V, RM, RMVB, VOB, DAT, DV, and many more common video formats.

Where can I download AudioCatch?
AudioCatch is available on the Mac App Store.
